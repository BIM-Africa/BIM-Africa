## Evidence and measurement

- The provided table shows 51 impressions for “website design mauritius”, 11 for “web design mauritius” and 10 for “web designers mauritius”, with no clicks on those rows. This supports prioritising the existing website service page; it does not prove a specific per-query rank or a particular cause of low CTR.
- The table includes 16 impressions for “branding company mauritius”. Confirm service availability before targeting it with sales content.
- Do not add totals across overlapping time windows.
- Compare equal 28-day periods, filtered to Mauritius and relevant commercial queries. Track the landing page, impressions, clicks, CTR, position and actual enquiry conversions together.
- Treat branded, commercial service, informational and portfolio/client queries separately when reporting progress.

## Content maintenance

1. Keep real project details, author attribution, contact information and company identity accurate.
2. Expand useful answers from actual enquiries: scope, content requirements, maintenance, integrations and quotation process. Avoid invented pricing, deadlines, offices, certifications or testimonials.
3. Publish additional content when it answers a different real customer question, with a contextual link to the relevant service.
4. Review Search Console after deployment and re-crawling; use observed query/page performance to prioritise further work.
5. Confirm whether Mauritius is the main market and which current services the company wants to sell before creating more country/service pages.
